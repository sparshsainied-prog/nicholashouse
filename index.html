Distant Suns (Continuum 1) · £12.99 signed

When the maiden voyage of the experimental ship Fortune goes catastrophically wrong, Dr. Olivia White and her small crew find themselves stranded in the far future and distant reaches of the galaxy.

With each use of its damaged light-drive, Fortune is propelled ever further into an unknowable future, forcing the crew to contend with their new reality, the danger it brings and the dark heart of the drive that caused it all.

Earth now a distant memory, Fortune and her crew venture onwards into an increasingly hostile and bizarre universe. But on their journey of survival, they will come to learn the ultimate fate of humanity and uncover a threat to the entire universe.

Nicholas

Nicholas House writes primarily science fiction and paranormal thrillers, but will turn his hand to whatever genre takes his interest.

He was born in Southern England and moved to North Wales young, where he started writing early: wizards and fantasy, inspired by Jules Verne, Greek mythology and the Welsh valleys, with some poetry on the side.

The mountains sent him outdoors and into Geoscience at university, and the travel that came with it gave him new cultures and landscapes to write from. Now in Oxfordshire with his wife and son, he has used his geology and his interest in space to write for an astronomy magazine and make scripted videos for his YouTube channel.
